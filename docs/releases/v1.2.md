# Clockchain v1.2

Released 2026-10-09 (UTC) by the owner's agent at the owner's instruction,
through the verified update lane ([OPERATIONS.md](../OPERATIONS.md) section 2),
under the owner decision recorded in [HOLD.md](../../HOLD.md) ("Owner decisions
— 2026-10-09"). Build `df094748ad64`.

## What changed in the node

- Seal route (PR #34): `GET /v1/seal` on the read boundary returns a stateless,
  node-signed seal over the current corpus digest, view commitment and
  candidate count ([SEALING.md](../design/SEALING.md) option A variant). The node
  signs with its existing receipt key; nothing is stored on the node, and the
  route is not part of the public gateway contract.
- No rule change: `fold_version` 1, the pinned manifest, `filter_version`, the
  curator set and `max_hops` are the ones bound at launch.

## What changed around it

- Seal log: the operator-held hash-chained log (`ops/seal_v1.py`,
  [OPERATIONS.md](../OPERATIONS.md) section 8) runs hourly from the owner's
  workstation against this build.
- Public read access: the gateway now also has a shared IPv4 address, and its
  origin is published in [PUBLIC-ACCESS.md](../PUBLIC-ACCESS.md). The
  verify block passed over IPv4 and IPv6 on 2026-10-09.
- Consumer integrations: the flag-gated read integrations listed in
  [INTEGRATIONS.md](../INTEGRATIONS.md) are merged with their flags off;
  enabling one is a separate owner step per repository.
- Documentation: the 2026-10-09 owner decisions (PR #37); the historical-truth
  evidence-standard wording (PR #33).

## Release evidence (private)

The update lane recorded: previous build `c56f73a89bcf`; exact-image acceptance
and the populated-update scenario on synthetic data; identity gate; corpus
digest, view commitment, export bytes and store fingerprint byte-identical before
and after the deploy; `provision-v1` a no-op on the populated store; verified
backups before and after. The commitment is unchanged since the owner's
2026-10-05 Delegate. The first build attempt failed before any production step
on a transient toolchain download error and was rerun.

## Known limits and follow-ups

- The seal log bounds rewrites of the served store; it does not rule them out,
  and external anchoring remains deferred.
- The gateway shares the node's read key; revoking the gateway alone needs a
  key rotation and a new owner decision.
- Every new subject still needs the curator (root) key for its Genesis and
  Delegate; a broader delegate scope is a design question, not scheduled.
- The 1973 claim, its influence edge and both images remain held; generation
  and model calls remain held. The v0 database is archived read-only and not
  dropped.
